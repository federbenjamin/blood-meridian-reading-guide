# Changelog

## v1.0 — 2026-06-24

First release.

- `Blood_Meridian_Vocabulary_Companion.epub`: the reading guide, about 1,500 terms chapter by chapter, each with a definition and its book quote.
- `Blood_Meridian_Dictionary_Extended.mobi`: a pop-up Kindle dictionary, Webster's 1913 with the book's ~1,300 special terms folded in.
- `Webster_1913_Dictionary.mobi` and `Blood_Meridian_Dictionary.mobi` in `dictionaries/`: Webster's 1913 alone, and the book's terms alone.
